Common demo inquiries

Sea Trial & walkthrough FAQs

What is a boat demo?

A boat demo (or sea trial) is an on-water test drive designed to let buyers evaluate hull performance, speed capabilities, engine noise, and ride comfort before completing their marine purchase.

How do I schedule a sea trial in Tampa Bay?

Simply submit your boat of interest and preferred scheduling window using our secure Riverview form. One of our marine advisers will contact you to verify qualifications and lock in your session.

What should I bring to a boat demo?

Please wear soft-soled non-marking boat shoes, protective sun apparel, and bring valid personal identification. We provide all necessary life vests and safety gear for all onboard guests.

Where do the boat trials take place?

Our sea trials launch from dedicated local docks on the Alafia River or near Hillsborough Bay, allowing you to test boats in both calm and open-water wave patterns.
