Our story

From a One-Bay Shop to Florida's Best

1980

The First Bay

RJ started turning wrenches out of a small garage bay in Riverview, prioritizing premium mechanical work and honest customer relationships above all else.

1997

Father & Son Expansion

Rob Koelovich joined his father full-time. The shop expanded into full sales, partnering with premium marine lines and starting our long relationship with Yamaha.

2026

Yamaha Excellence

Now a multi-franchise elite dealership, RJ's proudly holds the Yamaha Five-Star Excellence Award, serving thousands of satisfied boaters annually.
