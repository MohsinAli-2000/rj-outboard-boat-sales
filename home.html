More about us

RJ Koelovich started a small outboard repair shop off Old Fannin Road. When his son Rob joined in 1997, the shop grew into a full boat and motor dealership — same hands, same phone number, a lot more boats. Today RJ's is one of the most trusted marine dealers in the Jackson metro.
