The benchmark of innovation

Crafted in Indiana. Ripped in Mississippi.

Built in Elkhart, Indiana, Bennington pontoons represent the pinnacle of marine engineering. Utilizing a rattle-free construction technique and premium thick-gauge aluminum, these vessels offer an ultra-quiet ride even through heavy chop on the Ross Barnett Reservoir.
