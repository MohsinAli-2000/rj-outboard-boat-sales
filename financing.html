Frequently Asked Questions

What credit score do I need for approval?

Generally, marine lenders look for a credit score of 680 or higher. Excellent tier rates start at 740+. We do work with specialized lenders for sub-prime circumstances.

What documentation is required to finalize the loan?

Typically, you'll need standard proof of income (recent paystubs or tax returns), valid government ID, and vessel specifications with registration coordinates.

How long does the loan approval process take?

In most instances, our lenders return a written decision within 2 to 4 business hours. Complex structural transactions may require 24 hours.
