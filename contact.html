Driving Directions

From I-75 (North or South)

Take Exit 250 toward Riverview. Head East on Gibsonton Dr for approximately 1.5 miles, then turn North onto US-301 S. RJ's Outboard Marine will be on your right just past the Alafia River bridge.

From Brandon / Valrico

Head South on Brandon Blvd, turn South on Providence Rd, then connect West to Bloomingdale Ave. Head South down US-301 S. We are located just before the River crossroads on the East side of the highway.
